Workgroup Assistant Responsibilities and Benefits:
  1. Answer Questions: Actively participate in group discussions and presentations to help new employees solve work-related problems and quickly adapt to the work environment.
  2. Facilitate Communication: Communicate proactively, foster a positive team atmosphere, share creative content and professional knowledge related to the company platform, and improve the overall quality of the team.
  3. Maintain Order: Actively maintain the company image and ensure team order. Promote harmonious communication among members, provide necessary advice and support to new members, and build a more cohesive and efficient team.
  4. Share Work Results: Share screenshots of completed daily tasks and successful exit case studies, and invite subordinates to join the group.
  5. Foster a Positive Work Atmosphere: Encourage and motivate team members and other group members to share various work screenshots, fostering a positive collaborative atmosphere.
  6. Development Support: Guide employees to become familiar with and proficient in platform operation and work methods, encourage them to continuously improve to increase income, and guide team development.
